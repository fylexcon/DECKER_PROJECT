Introducing Atlas Series

December 30, 2025 | Category: Product Launch

We are thrilled to announce the launch of our newest innovation: The Atlas Series.

At Decker, we constantly strive to bridge the gap between nature and technology. The Atlas Series represents a significant leap forward in WPC (Wood Plastic Composite) technology.

What makes Atlas unique?

Featuring 3D deep embossed textures, the Atlas Series mimics real timber like never before. The grain patterns are deeper, more realistic, and offer a tactile experience that rivals natural wood.

  • High durability against scratches
  • Enhanced UV resistance
  • Zero maintenance required
