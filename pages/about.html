OUR STORY

Pioneering Composite Innovation Since 2011

It started with a simple idea: to create a decking material that offers the warmth of wood without the environmental impact or the constant maintenance. Today, Decker is a global leader in WPC (Wood Plastic Composite) solutions.

We operate fully integrated manufacturing facilities where recycled plastics and reclaimed wood fibers are transformed into high-performance products. Our commitment goes beyond manufacturing; it's about crafting lifestyles.
