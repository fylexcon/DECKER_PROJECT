Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Decker WPC products.

What is WPC by Decker?

Decker WPC (Wood-Plastic Composite) is a durable material made by combining wood fiber and high-quality plastics. It offers the natural look of wood while being resistant to water, rot, and insects.

What are the advantages of Decker WPC products?

Decker WPC products offer numerous benefits:
• Long-lasting and low maintenance
• Resistant to moisture, insects, and decay
• Eco-friendly and recyclable
• Natural wood appearance without cracking or warping

What is Decker WPC decking and where is it used?

Decker WPC decking is a composite flooring solution for outdoor spaces such as terraces, gardens, and pool areas. It combines the beauty of wood with high durability against harsh weather conditions.

What is Decker WPC wall cladding?

Decker WPC wall cladding is used to cover walls or building facades. It provides a decorative and protective solution that is water-resistant, UV-resistant, and impact-resistant.

What is a Decker WPC pergola and its benefits?

A Decker WPC pergola is an outdoor structure that provides shade and aesthetic appeal. Unlike traditional wood pergolas, it requires minimal maintenance and has a significantly longer lifespan without rotting.

Can Decker WPC products be used outdoors?

Yes. Decker WPC products are specifically designed for outdoor use. They are formulated to withstand rain, sun, snow, and temperature fluctuations without significant degradation.

Do Decker WPC products fade under sunlight?

Decker WPC products are UV-resistant and retain their color much longer than natural wood. While slight color stabilization occurs in the first few months, they do not turn gray like timber.

How do Decker WPC products compare to natural wood?

Compared to wood, Decker WPC is more durable, water-resistant, and insect-proof. It requires no staining or sealing, has no splinters, and offers a lower total cost of ownership over its lifespan due to minimal maintenance.

Are Decker WPC products fire-resistant?

Decker WPC products have a high fire resistance compared to natural wood, but they are not completely fireproof. Always follow safety guidelines when using fire pits or grills near the deck.

Can Decker WPC products be recycled?

Yes. Decker WPC products are eco-friendly and 100% recyclable. At the end of their lifespan, they can be reprocessed into new composite materials, contributing to a sustainable environment.
