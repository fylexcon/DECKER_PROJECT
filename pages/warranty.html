Structural Performance

"Structural performance" refers to the product's ability to retain load-bearing capacity, resistance to warping, cracking, splitting, or separation at panel joints. This warranty also covers structural issues resulting from material or workmanship defects originating from the factory.

Installation Requirements

Decker® WPC Wall Cladding products must be installed strictly in accordance with Decker®'s written standards and guidelines. The installation must use profiles, fasteners, and screws specified by Decker, and must be performed only by Decker® approved and authorized installation contractors.

Failure to follow these requirements, use of unauthorized materials, or improper installation techniques may negatively affect product performance and will void the warranty. This provision is implemented to protect Decker®'s quality standards and product safety.

Stain Protection

Decker WPC Wall Cladding products provide stain protection under normal usage and maintenance conditions, subject to the following guidelines:

  • Decker® Paris Model: Spilled substances such as oil, paint, or other heavy materials will not leave a stain on the panel if immediately wiped with a damp or soapy cloth.
  • Decker® Istanbul and Decker® Miami Models: Any heavy substances (e.g., oil) must be wiped off within 1 day using a cloth with Decker® recommended cleaning solution or soap. Delays beyond 1 day increase the risk of permanent staining.
  • Paint or Coatings Spills of paint or similar coating materials are not covered under this warranty, regardless of cleaning.

Fade Resistance

Decker® WPC Wall Cladding products are protected against color changes under normal usage and maintenance conditions.

Exterior Panels: For panels exposed to UV light, color change is limited to Delta E ≤ 10 during the first 5 years and Delta E ≤ 25 in subsequent years. Although the products are UV-protected, prolonged exposure to sunlight cannot be completely prevented from causing color changes.

Interior Panels: Interior panels are not affected by color changes; no color loss occurs under normal interior usage. Color changes resulting from improper use, inadequate maintenance, or extreme environmental conditions are not covered under this warranty.

Exposure to Heat

Products are designed for -20°C to +50°C (-4°F to 122°F) and 30-85% relative humidity. Extended exposure to temperatures outside these ranges may affect color and performance.
