Introducing the Atlas Series

We are thrilled to announce the launch of our newest innovation: The Atlas Series. A revolutionary step in composite engineering.

At Decker, we constantly strive to bridge the gap between nature and technology. The Atlas Series represents a significant leap forward in WPC (Wood Plastic Composite) technology, offering unparalleled aesthetics combined with industrial-grade durability.

What makes Atlas unique?

Featuring 3D deep embossed textures, the Atlas Series mimics real timber like never before. The grain patterns are deeper, more realistic, and offer a tactile experience that rivals natural wood. It isn't just about looks; it's about feeling the difference underfoot.

  • High Durability: Engineered to withstand scratches and heavy foot traffic.
  • Enhanced UV Resistance: New protective cap layer prevents fading even in direct sunlight.
  • Zero Maintenance: Requires no staining, sealing, or painting. Ever.
  • Eco-Friendly: Made from 95% recycled materials.
