CARE & MAINTENANCE

Expert tips to keep your Decker products looking brand new for years to come.

1. Washing Deck

WPC deck surfaces can be cleaned regularly to maintain their appearance and long-term performance. For light dirt and dust, rinsing with clean water is usually sufficient. For heavier dirt, warm water mixed with a mild, non-abrasive detergent may be used. During cleaning, a soft brush or cloth should be used. Harsh brushes, wire pads, and aggressive chemical cleaners that may damage the surface must be avoided.

If pressure washing is required, the water pressure should not exceed 1,200 PSI. The nozzle should not be held too close to the surface, and water should not be applied to the same area for an extended period. Excessive pressure may cause surface wear or deformation and is not recommended. After washing, allow the surface to dry naturally and ensure proper drainage to prevent water accumulation.

2. Dirty and Stains

Dirt and stains on WPC deck surfaces should be cleaned as soon as possible to prevent permanent marks. Common stains such as food, beverages, grease, or oil can usually be removed with warm water and a mild, non-abrasive detergent. For tougher stains, gently scrub the affected area with a soft brush or cloth, following the direction of the deck boards. Abrasive cleaners, solvent-based chemicals, bleach, or strong acids must not be used, as they may damage or discolor the surface.

Decker products are formulated to resist mold growth. However, despite this protection, prolonged exposure to moisture, dirt, and organic debris can still lead to mold or mold-like stains on the surface. To prevent this, it is recommended to clean the deck regularly, avoid water accumulation, and ensure proper air circulation. Mold stains that do appear can usually be removed easily with mild detergent and a soft brush if treated promptly.

3. Scratches & Surface Marks

The WPC deck surface combines a natural wood-like appearance with high durability. However, heavy furniture, hard-soled shoes, or moving objects can cause minor scratches or surface abrasion marks. These marks are generally part of normal usage and do not affect the performance of the product.

To minimize their appearance, place protective pads or felt under furniture and heavy items, and avoid direct contact of sharp or hard objects with the deck surface. With regular cleaning and simple precautions, the deck surface can maintain both its aesthetic appeal and long-lasting performance.

4. Removing Snow, Ice & Water

Snow, ice, and standing water on a WPC deck should be removed regularly to maintain both safety and aesthetic appearance. Use a plastic shovel or brush for snow and ice removal; metal shovels or sharp tools can damage the surface.

Salt or harsh chemicals should not be used to melt ice, as they may cause discoloration or surface deformation. Ensure proper drainage and water flow to prevent water from pooling on the deck for extended periods. Regular cleaning and proper handling help maintain the deck’s slip resistance and overall appearance.

5. Routine Inspection

Regular inspection and maintenance are essential for the long-lasting performance and safe use of a WPC deck. Periodic checks help preserve the deck’s aesthetic appearance and allow early detection of potential issues. Deck panels, screws, clips, and other fasteners should be inspected periodically, and any loose or damaged components should be repaired immediately.

Gaps between panels should be checked for water accumulation, dirt, or organic debris, and drainage channels should be kept clear. Any signs of cracks, scratches, deformation, or wear should be addressed promptly. During routine inspections, observe the general condition of the deck surface, including any slight discoloration or fading due to sun exposure. Regular maintenance minimizes potential repair costs over time.
