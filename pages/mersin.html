Decker Arrives in Mersin

Gateway to the Mediterranean

We are excited to establish our newest strategic hub in Mersin, Turkiye. Positioned at one of the Mediterranean's most vital trade intersections, this facility strengthens our distribution network across the region.

Mersin is not just a location; it is the heartbeat of logistics in southern Turkiye. By opening this center, Decker ensures that our premium WPC products—from the Atlas Series to our classic decking—are readily available for large-scale construction projects and architectural developments throughout Anatolia and beyond.

Why Mersin?

Our presence in this coastal metropolis allows us to deliver efficiency alongside quality. The salt-rich air and humid climate of the Mediterranean also provide the perfect testing ground for our weather-resistant composite technology.

  • Strategic Logistics: Utilizing Mersin's major port capabilities for rapid international and domestic shipping.
  • Regional Hub: Immediate access to stock for contractors along the southern coast.
  • Coastal Durability: Proving our products' resistance to humidity and saline environments.
  • Showroom Access: A dedicated space for clients to view our full color and texture range.
